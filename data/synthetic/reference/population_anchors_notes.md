Bu referans dosyası, sentetik v3 hastane kohortunun kalibrasyonu için Sağlık Bakanlığı, SGK, TÜİK, OECD ve Eurostat kaynaklarından derlenen `119` kamu istatistiği satırını `117` tekil çıpa (`anchor`) altında birleştirir.

## 3. Caveats

1. **2010 MEDULA data is old:** The patient-level age (`age_mix_inpatients_sgk_nonmoh_2010`), sex (`sex_mix_inpatients_sgk_nonmoh_2010`), TMA operation-group (`operations_by_tma_group_2010`), and non-MoH ICD-10 chapter (`icd10_chapter_shares_inpatients_sgk_nonmoh_2010`, `alos_by_icd10_chapter_2010`) distributions come from `2,255,836` stays in 2010 across 491 non-MoH hospitals (`alos_all_sgk_nonmoh_hospitals_2010`, mean `3.93` days, median `2` days). Treat them as structural priors rather than current 2024–2025 volume levels.
2. **"Özel" (private) sector scope:** In the MoH yearbook (Chapter 8 note, p.208), the `Özel` column includes private hospitals affiliated with foundation universities (whereas foundation-university teaching centres — Vakıf Üniversitesi SUAM — are under `Üniversite`). A further historical note states that services of other public institutions are shown in the private sector for comparability.
3. **Tablo 8.6 percentages are column shares (each sector's share of a group's national total), NOT row shares:** In `siy24_tbl86_published_pct_sector_share_of_each_group_2024`, private hospitals account for `21.1%` of group A, `20.4%` of group B, and `25.4%` of group C nationally (`22.9%` of all operations). The operation-group mix *within* private hospitals is given in `siy24_tbl86_group_mix_within_sector_derived_2024` (`13.8%` A, `32.1%` B, `54.1%` C). By contrast, Tablo 7.3 (`siy24_tbl73_icu_type_mix_pct_by_sector_2024`) prints row percentages (ICU type mix within each sector).
4. **Yearbook A/B/C operation groups vs SUT A1–E groups:** The MoH yearbook (`siy24_group_definition_operations_ABC`) defines operation groups A, B, and C using the WHO International Classification of Health Interventions (ICHI) and excludes diagnostic and minor surgical procedures. It gives no D or E group and no mapping to the SUT / TMA billing groups (`A1`–`A3`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `E` in `operations_by_tma_group_2010`); do not equate the two classifications.
5. **"Ortalama kalış günü" is days; its relation to nights is not stated:** The MoH yearbook (`siy24_alos_published_by_sector_2024`) defines `Ortalama Kalış Günü` as `yatılan gün sayısı / (taburcu + ölen)` (`bed-days / inpatients`). The sources read do not say how a same-day stay or the day of discharge is counted, so do not equate these days (`2.7` in private hospitals, `4.2` overall in 2024) with nights (discharge date minus admission date) without checking the counting rule.
6. **Figures are stay-based (or visit/application-based), not person-based:** Inpatient counts (`Yatan Hasta Sayısı` = discharged + deceased in the year), physician visits (`Hekime Müracaat Sayısı`), and SGK MEDULA applications (`Hastane Müracaat Sayısı`) count episodes/contacts, not unique patients. Repeated admissions or visits by the same person within the year are counted separately.
