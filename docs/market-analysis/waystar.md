# Waystar (NASDAQ: WAY)

## Summary

- **$791M 2023 revenue, 19% YoY growth** — mature and growing RCM platform
- **IPO'd June 2024 raising $968M** — validates RCM venture category
- **30K+ customers, 1M+ providers** served
- **108.8% Net Revenue Retention** — strong customer expansion signal
- **40%+ EBITDA margin** sustained over 3 years — operational discipline
- **Serves 18 of 22 U.S. News top hospitals** — elite-tier penetration

### Customer base
- **30K+ customers**
- **1M+ providers** served
- **18 of 22 U.S. News top hospitals** as customers
- Platform spans end-to-end RCM: eligibility, claim processing, payment, denial management, patient billing

### Product positioning
- End-to-end cloud-based RCM software platform
- AI/ML features layered across: claim scrubbing, denial prediction, eligibility verification, patient payment estimation
- Not a point solution — positions as full-stack RCM platform

### Pricing model
- Enterprise SaaS with tiered packages based on claim volume + modules
- Typical mid-size hospital deal: **$50K-$500K/year** for core modules <!-- unverified: pricing tiers not publicly disclosed; estimated from contract-database hearsay -->
- Large health system enterprise deals: **$1M-$10M+ ARR** <!-- unverified -->
- Contingency pricing available for specific denial-recovery products

## Relevance to ILAY

- **Validates RCM venture category** at scale: public comp demonstrates RCM software can build billion-dollar businesses
- **NRR of 108.8%** = proof that RCM customers expand share-of-wallet over time (supports ILAY's hybrid wedge-then-expand model)
- **ILAY cannot compete directly with Waystar in the US** — Waystar owns enterprise relationships, Epic integrations, payer connectivity
- **Waystar has NO Turkey presence** (no MEDULA integration) — Turkey is structurally protected from Waystar entry
- **Lesson**: full-stack RCM platforms outperform point solutions; ILAY should aim for platform breadth over time, not just SUT claim validation
- **Benchmark pricing**: Waystar's tiers anchor what large Turkish chains might pay if ILAY positions as enterprise platform (₺2-5M/year group-wide contracts credible)
